# Simulation 02: Intermittent Wi-Fi Connectivity

## Environment
- Platform: ServiceDesk Simulator
- Practice type: Simulated service desk ticket
- Ticket: NET17923487388120
- User: Daniel Reed
- Department: Finance
- Status: Resolved following user-confirmed recovery

## Reported Issue and Business Impact
Daniel reported that his Wi-Fi disconnected every few seconds.
His video call had dropped three times, preventing him from
maintaining a stable meeting connection.

## Investigation and User Communication
During the support conversation, Daniel reported that his
connection had returned and appeared stable. He described the
interruption as a brief outage.

Followed up to verify whether the connection continued dropping.
Daniel confirmed there were no further drops.

## Actions Taken
- Communicated with the user about the connection's current state.
- Verified stability through follow-up rather than relying only
  on the initial report that connectivity had returned.
- Avoided unnecessary configuration changes after the user
  confirmed recovery.

No technician-performed network changes were made.

## Outcome and Verification
Daniel confirmed that the connection remained stable with no
further drops during the follow-up.

This was a recovery confirmed by the user. The underlying cause
was not established, and a wider network outage was not independently
verified.

## Resolution Summary
User reported that Wi-Fi connectivity returned during the support
conversation. Follow-up confirmed no further connection drops.
No configuration changes were required. Root cause remains unconfirmed.

## Lessons Learned
- Verify that the user's work can continue before closing a ticket.
- A connection recovering does not establish why it failed.
- Record user observations separately from confirmed technical findings.
- Make changes when the evidence supports them.

## Skills Practiced
- Clear user communication
- Assessing business impact
- Verifying service recovery
- Accurate resolution notes
- Distinguishing symptoms, recovery, and root cause
